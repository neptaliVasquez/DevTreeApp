[Catalog] [DIS] Finalise HDR UK 2-way syndication testing
 
Background
 
ADDI exchanges dataset metadata with the HDR UK Innovation Gateway in both directions
through DIS:
 
- Pull (HDR UK → GRIP): the hdruk provider fetches the ADDI collection (Gateway id 143) and
  maps it to GRIP format. GRIP's syndication cron then ingests it.
- Push (GRIP → HDR UK): the grip-hdruk provider serves level-0 datasets ("Local data access",
  ADWB catalogue). HDR UK's federation harvester pulls them on its own schedule.
